Your own robot on X that helps with city stuff.

Mention your robot from your X account: "pothole at 5th & Main", "weather", "next train". It finds the right city service and answers you, from its own account, only for you.

Get your robot (about 10 minutes)

Free while in testing. You keep control: pause it, disconnect it or delete everything at any time.

📍 Reports problems to the city

Potholes, broken streetlights, graffiti… Your robot finds which 311 system covers the spot (Open311 or SeeClickFix) and prepares the report. It only files after you reply YES, and only if you switch sending on.

🚌 Answers quick questions

Add skills from a reviewed list: weather, next trains and buses, recent 311 activity nearby. More cities over time.

🔒 It's your robot

It's a separate X account that you own, labeled as automated and linked to you. It replies only to you. Say STOP and it stops.

How it works

  1. Sign in with X using your normal account, so we know who you are. We don't keep access to it.
  2. Make a robot account: a second X account just for your robot. We walk you through it, including X's "Automated" label.
  3. Connect the robot: sign in to X as the robot and allow civicbot to read its mentions and reply.
  4. Pick your city and skills, then mention your robot from your account.

Good to know

  • Your robot only replies to posts that mention it. It never posts on its own, never DMs and never follows anyone.
  • Many US towns don't have a 311 API yet. We'll tell you up front; weather and transit skills still work.
  • civicbot is an independent open-source project, in testing. Read the privacy notice (draft).
