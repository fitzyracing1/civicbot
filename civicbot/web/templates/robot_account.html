Step 2: Make a separate X account for your robot

Hi @{{ owner.handle }}! X's rules say bots need their own account, clearly labeled as automated. Your personal account stays just yours.

Do this in X (about 5 minutes)

  1. Sign out of X, or open a private/incognito window, and create a new account at x.com. Pick a name like @{{ owner.handle[:9] }}_robot. You'll need an email or phone number that isn't already on your personal account.
  2. In the robot's profile bio, say it's a bot and who runs it, e.g. "Robot run by @{{ owner.handle }}. Helps with 311 & city info."
  3. Still signed in as the robot, go to Settings and privacy → Your account → Account information → Automation (direct link: x.com/settings/account/automation). Choose Managing account, enter @{{ owner.handle }}, then confirm by signing in as @{{ owner.handle }}. The robot's profile will then show "Automated by @{{ owner.handle }}".

Don't see "Automation"? X is still rolling the label out. Finish the other steps; your robot's bio already says it's a bot. Check again later.
