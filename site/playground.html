Playground

Ask the way you would @mention your robot on X. Your browser calls free public APIs directly (CORS), so there is no server, no account, no key and no tracking. Nothing is posted anywhere, and 311 reports are only ever a dry run.

Questions without “in <place>” use this.

Data comes from Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0), US NWS, USGS, NOAA, Frankfurter/ECB, Nager.Date, Wikipedia (CC BY-SA), Zippopotam.us, openFDA, NHTSA, TfL, MBTA and wheretheiss.at. Please be gentle: the robot waits 1 s between questions. The real robot uses the same free APIs through reviewed skill specs (directory).
